# Carom

Carom is a fast-paced, arcade-style paddle duel that runs entirely in your browser. Two paddles face off across a dark field while a ball ricochets between them, bouncing off walls and obstacles in the middle. The defining mechanic is spin: move your paddle as you strike the ball to curve its flight path and set up devastating bank shots.

In Carom, every paddle swing matters. Strike the ball at the top of your paddle to send it upward, at the bottom to send it downward, and the motion of your paddle at contact adds spin that curves the ball mid-flight. This spin mechanic turns the open field into a puzzle—use the two obstacles in the middle to bank shots around your opponent's defense. First to 11 points wins, with a 2-point lead required at deuce.

Play against a tactical AI opponent in Solo mode or challenge a friend on the same keyboard in Versus mode. The game is fully playable without any installation, backend, or API—everything runs in your browser, generating graphics and sound in real-time with no external assets.
